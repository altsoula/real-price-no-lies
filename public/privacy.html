Privacy Policy, Cookies & Affiliate Disclosure

Effective Domain: https://realpricenolies.online · Last Updated: November 2026

1. Zero Personal Information Collected

RealPriceNoLies requires no user accounts, usernames, email addresses, passwords, or payment details. Product URLs submitted for inspection automatically have common tracking parameters (`utm_*`, `fbclid`, `gclid`, `ref`, `tag`, `pf_rd_*`, etc.) stripped before lookup.

2. Rate Limiting, Caching & Aggregate Counters

To prevent automated abuse and control API expenses, our server evaluates short-lived IP rate-limit windows using one-way SHA-256 hashes inside temporary cache buckets that expire automatically within minutes. Cached price reports store public product price metadata (never user identities), and public traffic counters record only anonymous aggregate totals.

3. Cookies, Analytics & Google AdSense

  • Strictly Necessary Storage: Preserves your consent settings (`rpnl_consent`) and light/dark UI theme (`rpnl_theme_v1`).
  • Privacy-Safe Traffic Analytics: When enabled, cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics or consent-gated Plausible metrics measure aggregate traffic volume without cross-site behavioral profiling.
  • Advertising Partners: When Google AdSense (`ads.txt` / partner ad units) is enabled and you opt into Advertising cookies in the Cookie Manager, Google and third-party vendors may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can adjust preferences anytime via "Manage Cookie Preferences".

4. Affiliate Links & Editorial Independence

RealPriceNoLies participates in retail affiliate programs (such as Amazon Associates / PartnerNet, Awin, and Sovrn Commerce). When you click an outbound `"Check Offer"` or `"View Store"` link (`rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`) and complete a qualifying purchase, RealPriceNoLies may earn a small commission at zero extra cost to you.

Zero Score Manipulation: Partner status never boosts, hides, or alters a retailer's listed price or a product's 0–100 Deal Truth Score.

5. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

Questions about privacy, sponsorship, or data practices can be sent to hello@realpricenolies.online or via the Contact & Sponsor Page.
