MISSION & METHODOLOGY

Why We Built RealPriceNoLies

Across major e-commerce stores—from Amazon and Otto to MediaMarkt, Saturn, IKEA, Zalando, and H&M—promotional banners routinely quote inflated "Was / UVP / MSRP" anchors to manufacture artificial urgency. Shoppers are forced to guess whether "-40% OFF" is a historic bargain or just yesterday's normal street price dressed in red paint.

RealPriceNoLies gives anyone an instant, account-free audit in seconds: paste a public product link, strip away tracking clutter, verify live shelf pricing alongside Google-Search-grounded market intelligence, and summarize everything on a blunt 0–100 Deal Truth Score.

How Every Verdict Is Weighted

  • vs. 90-Day Market Average (35 pts): Benchmarks today's offer against typical rolling 3-month street prices rather than promotional list prices.
  • 180-Day Low / High Position (30 pts): Measures how close the current price sits to the lowest verified price recorded over the past 6 months.
  • Cross-Retailer Spread (20 pts): Checks competing retailers carrying the same model/SKU so you immediately see cheaper alternatives.
  • UVP & Promo Honesty Audit (15 pts): Penalizes pre-sale anchor hikes and exaggerated fake strike-through discounts.

How RealPriceNoLies Stays 100% Free

We do not sell user profiles, require registrations, or collect email lists. Infrastructure and AI research credits are funded transparently through consent-gated display ads, direct sponsor placements, and optional affiliate links on outbound store comparisons—none of which ever influence a product's 0–100 score.
